Origins & Metier

Made in Sialkot: The Alchemy of Metal and Bloom

Renowned internationally for surgical metallurgy and bespoke leather artistry, Sialkot is also home to Pakistan’s most secretive cadre of master distillers and flacon sculptors.

Each SCANDAL vessel undergoes seventeen manual processes. The iconic high-heeled legs cap is cast from solid virgin brass, hand-buffed to a mirror gold sheen, and fitted atop heavy lead-free crystal annealed in cantonment furnaces. Inside, organic night gardenia petals and honey accord age gracefully in dark oak casks.
